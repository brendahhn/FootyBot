## 🔬 Deep dive — "What's the deal with CeeDee and Pickens?" You already have the answer: 2025.

Your mock memo asked it twice — *"CeeDee without George Pickens?"* and *"only want Pickens + Lamb
if they're a real 1-2, not competing for the same targets."* The premise needs a correction that
happens to resolve the whole question: **the Pickens-to-Dallas trade was May 2025**, so Lamb and
Pickens **already shared a full season together.** This isn't a projection — it's evidence.

**What actually happened in 2025 [A]:**
- In their **12 full games together**, both were startable *at the same time* — Lamb led targets
  116–99 and PPR 16.6–16.1. Defenses couldn't double both.
- Full lines: **Pickens 93/1,429/9** (career year, Pro Bowl, 2nd-team All-Pro); **Lamb 75/1,077/3**
  on 117 targets despite **missing 3 games** (Week-3 high-ankle sprain).
- Pipeline cross-check [S]: under our scoring **Pickens finished WR6 (14.44 PPG) and Lamb WR11
  (12.57 PPG) simultaneously.** Coexistence, confirmed by the numbers, not the narrative.
- The one real cannibalization signal: Lamb's targets fell from 10+/gm (2024) to 8.4/gm. **Pickens
  caps Lamb's ceiling — not his value.**

**Verdict:**
- **CeeDee Lamb — BUY, a Round-1 WR (WR5-6 tier).** And note a hidden tailwind: he scored just
  **3 TDs on 1,077 yards** — abnormally low, i.e. positive TD regression on top of a rock-solid
  28%-target-share role. For you at pick 4 he's a legitimate on-the-board name.
- **George Pickens — BUY, value at a WR2 price (~WR12-20 ADP).** A 1,400-yard All-Pro who stayed
  a WR1/high-WR2 even *with* Lamb, now in a contract year (franchise tag, wants the long deal).
- **Are they a real 1-2? Yes — with evidence.** Drafting both is defensible.

**The failure mode, named [A]:** the entire thesis rests on **Dak Prescott's ~600-attempt volume
and his health.** He turns 33, missed most of 2024 with a hamstring, and the backups (Milton/Howell)
are a cliff. If you draft **both** Lamb and Pickens you are **doubling down on one fragile QB
ecosystem** — if Dak goes down, both crater together. That's a correlated bet, not a diversified
one. Secondary risk: Schottenheimer's stated push toward more run balance (career 46% run) could
trim the pass pie in Year 2. (Full new Cowboys entry written to `research/coach-tendencies.md`
