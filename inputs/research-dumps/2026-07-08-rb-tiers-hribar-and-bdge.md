## SOURCE 1 — Sharp Football RB tiers (Rich Hribar), 2026

Framing note from the author worth keeping: **tiers ≠ rankings ≠ projections ≠ ADP.** His tiers
are archetype/how-points-are-accrued groupings meant to expose pricing arbitrage, NOT a linear
ranking. Useful lens for our Lane C countdown.

- **Tier 1:** Jahmyr Gibbs, Bijan Robinson — do-it-all, front-of-age-curve, 400-pt-PPR ceiling.
  Edges Gibbs (better offense/Vegas; Lions favored in 14, 3rd-most; Detroit projected 3rd in
  scoring vs ATL 25th). Both apex "win-your-league" backs. Gibbs OL in flux (Ragnow retired,
  Glasgow released; Sewell to LT; rookie Blake Miller RT). Bijan: more workload runway (Allgeier
  gone), red-zone usage is the growth area (RZ fantasy finishes 13/6/23 to open career).
- **Tier 2 (own tier):** Christian McCaffrey — highest upside + most red flags. RB1/RB2 overall
  every time he's played 10+ games. 2025: 413 touches (led NFL), 2,126 scrimmage, 17 TD, 102
  catches. BUT turns 30, 450 touches incl. playoffs (only Murray '14, Barkley '24, Foster '12
  had more since 2010 — ALL declined + lost touches the next year). Rushing efficiency cratered
  (39th YPC 3.9, 1.10 YBC/att career low). Big Kittle-on/off splits (4.1 vs 3.6 YPC). SF thin at
  RB behind him. **This corroborates our own newsletter call: CMC's risk is workload/age, not
  the stale "post-injury" label.**
- **Tier 3 (weekly RB1 upside, one blemish):** James Cook, Saquon Barkley, Jonathan Taylor,
  Omarion Hampton, Chase Brown, Kenneth Walker, Derrick Henry, Josh Jacobs.
  - Cook: career-high 309 att/1,621/14 TD; 2nd YPC(5.2), 2nd stuff rate; underused as receiver
    (2.0 rec/gm); new OC Pete Carmichael (Payton tree) → possible passing-game bump. Win/loss
    splits stark (19.9 in wins vs 10.3 in losses); Bills favored in 14.
  - Saquon: 1,413/9 in a "disappointing" 2025; YPC 5.8→4.1, YBC/att 2.64→1.36 (banged-up OL,
    Stoutland gone, Mannion more under-center); Hurts vulturing goal line (Tush Push); RB14-ish.
    A.J. Brown gone → possible target bump (17.3% route tgt w/o Brown vs 14.7% with).
  - J. Taylor: 369 touches, 1,963 scrimmage, league-high 20 TD, 84.4% backfield share; but
    strong win/loss + Daniel Jones-return volatility (RB21/29/28/18 in Jones's full-game losses).
  - Hampton (LAC): injury-shortened rookie but explosive when clean (7.4 YPC hit-beyond-line,
    7th); Mike McDaniel OC + healthier OL = big Year-2 boost; hardest rushing schedule though.
  - Chase Brown (CIN): RB2 overall after Burrow returned Wk13; you're buying the environment
    (20.2 PPR/gm in Burrow full games); Bengals favored in 15 (2nd-most). Efficiency shaky
    (35.8% success, 37th) but role/volume elite; only competes with Perine.
  - K. Walker (KC): new KC contract → biggest workload of career; explosive (12.1% 10+yd rate
    since '22, 2nd to Cook 12.5%); KC RBs historically great light-box rate under Mahomes; but
    passing-down/pass-pro concerns (Bieniemy wants 3-down); Mahomes ACL return could mean more
    run early.
  - D. Henry (BAL): age-32, still elite (322 touches, 1,745, 16 TD, 8th straight 10+ TD season);
    Ravens favored in 14; no passing role (better in non-PPR).
  - Jacobs (GB): 270 touches, 1,211, 14 TD; 5th in RZ fantasy points; knee injury tanked 2nd
    half; **non-zero suspension risk from a May domestic dispute (no charges yet)** — falling to
    R3/R4 turn; would be R2/R3 without the cloud. High TD equity + healthier OL.
- **Tier 4 (talent = Tier 3, but bad team environment caps ceiling):** Ashton Jeanty, De'Von
  Achane, Breece Hall, Jeremiyah Love. (All on teams with ≤5.5 win totals; historical note: top-3
  RB scorers since 1990 avg 9.9-win teams.)
  - Jeanty (LV): disappointing rookie efficiency (3.7 YPC, 30.8% success — 49th) behind a
    disaster OL, but explosive when clean (6.8 YPC hit-beyond-line); OL retooled (Linderbaum,
    Miller/Powers-Johnson back); 84.3% backfield share; low QB bar (Cousins/Mendoza).
  - Achane (MIA): RB5 in PPG (20.2) but heavy Tua-dependence (20.3 PPG w/ Tua vs 10.5 w/o); now
    Malik Willis (mobile, low RB-target rate 10.2%, high scramble) → downgrades his receiving
    upside; MIA "worst environment in NFL," favored in 1. Volatile RB2.
  - Breece Hall (NYJ): good counting stats/bad environment poster boy (RB17/RB19 last two yrs);
    Jets favored in 4; low goal-line usage.
  - **Jeremiyah Love (ARI, rookie, 2026 1st):** class's crown jewel — led class in YAC/att (4.2),
    10.0 YPC when hit beyond line (best for 100+att back in 10 yrs), 22.2% route target rate;
    lands in a bad ARI offense (favored in 0, projected fewest points), Allgeier/Conner clash for
    goal line. History: top-10-drafted RBs since 2010 almost all finished RB1 in PPG as rookies.
    Hribar expects a Bijan-like usable-out-of-the-box role. **High-value rookie flag.**
- **Tier 5 (RB2 tag, weekly RB1 upside; good for WR-heavy-early builds — i.e. BRENDAN):** Kyren
  Williams, Travis Etienne, Javonte Williams, D'Andre Swift, David Montgomery, Tony Pollard.
  - Kyren (LAR): rock-steady (3 straight 1,300+/10+TD) but now a drive-by-drive split w/ Corum
    (59.3% backfield share late); Rams favored in league-high 16, top projected offense; TD
    regression (73% of Rams TDs were passing) should favor Kyren's RZ role.
  - Etienne (NO): rebound 1,399/13 but TD-dependent; Kamara still around (looked motivated at
    OTAs) → threat to workload; NO OL was decimated (31st run-block PFF); great fantasy schedule
    (dome, NFC South).
  - Javonte (DAL): bounce-back 1,338/13, new 3yr/$24M deal; huge goal-line role (82.4% of snaps
    inside-10, 5th; 18 of 23 inside-5 touches); but awful receiving (3.9 YPC on catches — 2nd
    lowest ever for 25+ rec); Jaydon Blue speculated for passing role; cooled late (1 RB1 week in
    final 8). DAL OL all back but injury-prone.
  - Swift (CHI): Ben Johnson lifted CHI run game (6th YPC, 2nd success); split w/ Monangai; career
    highs; but Dalman retired + Trapilo (LT) may miss year → some OL regression.
  - Montgomery (HOU, traded): early-down grinder/TD-based RB2; man-gap scheme fit (led all backs
    in EPA/rush on man-gap); HOU OL rebuilt; Woody Marks pushed down.
  - Pollard (TEN): arbitrage/cheap; 5 straight 1,000-yd seasons but low TDs (RB23/21/31 in PPG);
    upside tied to TEN offense jump under Cam Ward/Daboll.
- **Tier 6 (young/trendy w/ red flags):** Cam Skattebo (NYG — gruesome ankle, expected camp-ready,
  bellcow role when healthy: 73.3% backfield share, 86.4% RZ snaps in his 4 pre-injury games),

- **Tiers 7-13 (committees / handcuffs / dart throws):** TreVeyon Henderson & Rhamondre Stevenson
