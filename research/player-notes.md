## James Cook (RB, BUF) — nuance on the "overvalued" take

Our 2026-07-08 newsletter agreed with Brendan that Cook is overvalued (market bid him to RB6;
he was RB6 at 16.81 PPG in 2025 under our scoring — priced at ceiling). **Counterpoint added from
Brendan's dump (2026-07-08):** Cook faced a light box (6- defenders) on only **11.3% of runs
(3rd-lowest in NFL)** yet still ranked **#2 YPC (5.2), #2 stuff rate (10.7%), #6 EPA/rush (+0.08),
#8 in 10+ yd run rate (13%)** [B, analyst-cited]. Read: his efficiency came against STACKED boxes,
so the talent is real — the "overvalued" case is *"priced at his ceiling / TD-dependent Buffalo
profile,"* NOT *"empty volume."* Possible upside lever: new OC Pete Carmichael (Sean Payton tree)
could finally use Cook as a receiver (he's at just 2.0 rec/gm). Net: still don't reach at 17, but
don't dismiss the player — if the passing role materializes he has top-3 RB upside.
